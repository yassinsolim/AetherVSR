## 4. Counter alignment

Common native analogue: `100*(G + deltaR)/deltaP`, where G is the sum of positive
metadata gaps, P is rVFC `presentedFrames`, and R is playback-quality dropped
frames. A/B have no RuntimeSession. Native callbacks K, boundary snapshots,
PTS, readiness, quality counts and timestamps are retained. Every complete
trial satisfies `deltaP - deltaK - G = 0` without repairing the residual.

C/D additionally retain the historical capture formula using RuntimeSession
skips/presented and boundary-read quality drops, and the exact RuntimeSession
formula using its accumulated `decoderDrops`. Session quality is sampled on
post-submit frames, not refreshed by snapshot(). These formulas happen to agree
in all eight active windows; their definitions remain distinct.

Independent verification reconciled554718 valid native rows and280051 active
submission rows. All eight native/runtime presented, gap and submission alignment
residuals were zero; all attempt-minus-submission deficits were zero. Attempts
are actual tick entries; submissions are successful queue-submit/session records,
not GPU completion or physical presentation. Imports are not separately counted.

First/last120s use half-open native callback-entry and runtime post-submit-entry
slices. Their first presented delta may span the preceding slice boundary.
Unavailable B runtime slice metrics were corrected from zeros to null after
capture; raw files, native metrics, active metrics and decisions are unchanged.
Potential counter overlap is not deduplicated. Neither P nor R is a count of
unique physically lost frames; quality drops are not decoder-only. See the
[counter audit](M10.5-COUNTERS.md) and [raw-indexed results](../results/m106-floor.json).

## 12. Practical-equivalence analysis

The actual test is **one-sided practical noninferiority**, not symmetric equality:
upper95% penalty<=0.5pp and<=0.5fps, frozen before data. These engineering
allowances are not perceptual guarantees or preservation of the old1% ceiling.
Four of six bounds fail: B loss/cadence and C/D loss. C/D cadence bounds pass.
No active penalty lower bound exceeds its0.5 margin, so Case B is not established.

Case C's six-condition conjunction is an intersection-union decision; Case B's
alternative component bounds are not familywise95% coverage. Non-significance
is not equivalence. One active late-window safety failure and unknown relevant
observer distortion independently block Case C. **Registered result: CASE D.**
