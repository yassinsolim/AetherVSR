integration differences. Original root harness layouts are not matched.

Paired M10-minus-M9 FPS differences: -0.866,-2.731,+0.367,+0.032. Source audit
found added fatal/disposal guards but unchanged model, graph, shaders, importer,

Extension-minus-matched-harness differences: +5.397,+2.133,+2.536,+4.368fps.
This is a consistent observed integration contrast favoring the extension in
these four blocks, not proof that the adapter itself is more efficient. There

Neural-minus-baseline FPS differences: -0.036,-0.430,+0.102,-0.398; combined-loss
differences: -0.054,+1.285,-0.216,+1.221 percentage points. Baseline was cheaper

neither is a robust unique-playback-loss benefit from switching to baseline.
**No RuntimeController change is justified.** No FPS-triggered fallback, threshold,
model, graph, precision or ingest change is made.

## Scheduling and instrumentation

No >=50ms long-task entry was captured in the41 scheduling-observed short runs;
three lean runs intentionally lack that observer. This does not exclude shorter
stalls, cross-thread scheduling delays or compositor behavior. Loop/seek/waiting
media events are retained in local raw and counted in compact summaries; they
are not integrity violations merely because playback crosses a loop boundary.

Owned-minus-standard FPS differences: +0.565,+0.333,-1.731. Lean-minus-standard:
+1.498,+0.099,-2.234. Instrumentation neutrality is **not established**. Owned
wrapped-callback occupancy was17.83,18.84,17.09ms/s in three trials, including
shared core and recorder work. It is not total adapter/renderer CPU; categories
can nest and must not be summed. QueueMicrotask callbacks are covered, Promise
continuations are not. No result is overhead-subtracted into a passing number.

Evidence: [comparison](../results/m105-comparison.json), with all44 rows, actual
browser records, source/media/case hashes, boundaries and local-raw references.
Independent review approved the arithmetic/retention and cautioned against
causal, equivalence and instrumentation-neutrality claims.

## Remaining causality question

Observed callback deficits exist before runtime attachment; shader cost alone
cannot explain them. Runtime submission failure did not explain active-arm gaps.
Browser/media callback/composition scheduling is implicated at the observation
boundary, but the exact internal mechanism and unique physical loss remain
**unknown**. GC, thermal throttling and GPU saturation are not established.

Next diagnostic is a separate12s Chrome trace within each20s observation window
on bare, matched-harness and extension-neural pages, categories media/blink,
32MiB trace buffer. Retain local raw/hash/recipe only. Trace events may correlate
current-frame updates, callback batches and published drop batches; they cannot
deduplicate the JS loss counters or supply GPU execution/scanout measurements.
This is not final performance acceptance. M10 remains EXTENSION MVP PARTIAL.
