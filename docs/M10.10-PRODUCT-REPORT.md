## 3. Product Semantics: Mirror vs Replay

Mirror means PAGE_AUTHORITY: page buffering, source selection, ABR and timeline
remain canonical. Replay means PLAYER_AUTHORITY: the controlled player owns an
independent timeline. Acquiring bytes does not grant control of the original
player or justify automatically pausing/muting it. A separate explicit reversible
action would be required. Neither model is shipping-qualified by this study.
