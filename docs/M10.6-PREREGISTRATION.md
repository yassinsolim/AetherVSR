## Hypothesis and statistical unit

H1: the <=1% historical combined-loss threshold is not reliably achievable by
the underlying Chrome HTML5-video path on this exact apparatus without AetherVSR
frame processing. The study attempts to falsify H1, not presume normalization.
Independent fresh-profile sessions are run units; four position-balanced blocks
provide four paired differences per treatment. Frames/callbacks are not replicates.

Report all valid runs, block-paired deltas, mean, standard deviation, min/max and
one-sided95% t bounds (df3,critical2.3533634348); also report two-sided95% intervals
(critical3.1824463053). Small-sample bounds assume independent approximately
normal block contrasts; show actual contrasts and do not claim population-wide
proof. No p>0.05 equality claim, outlier deletion or frame-level pseudoreplication.
Unexpected dependence, drift or apparatus mismatch that undermines inference
forces Case D, not additional favorable sampling.
The assumptions also apply to native-A mean bounds. With four blocks they cannot
be reliably diagnosed from a normality test. All six noninferiority conditions
for Case C form an intersection-union decision; Case B's individual bounds are
componentwise95%, not a familywise95% association claim.

Commit this reviewed protocol before any M10.6 observer, proxy, public or primary
observations. Freeze the tested apparatus commit/hash before the corresponding
measurement phase. Smoke tests verify instrumentation only and cannot enter result
tables. Any result-affecting apparatus correction requires affected reruns and a
recorded reason, not a changed threshold or undisclosed replacement.

## Practical margin and cases

Predeclare one-sided practical noninferiority limits: treatment-minus-A L <=
**0.5 percentage points**, and A native callback FPS minus treatment submission
FPS (B uses native callback FPS) <=**0.5fps**. Negative differences are not harm;
this is a degradation region, not symmetric equality. Require the upper95%
bound, not merely the estimate, inside each limit. Report90% two-sided intervals
as the equivalent upper-bound presentation, not non-significance as equivalence.

Rationale:0.5pp spends at most half the original1% score budget; at36k presented
frames it permits180 additional aggregate counter increments over600s, not180
unique lost frames.0.5fps is0.83% of60fps and at most300 submissions over600s.
These are conservative engineering tolerances, not psychophysical visibility
thresholds. M10.5 neural-baseline differences[-0.430,+0.102]fps and observed
baseline spread0.698fps justify measuring uncertainty, not widening this bound.
Bare callback spread49.096-59.295fps warns that Case D is plausible; do not
increase margins to fit that variance. For C/D require zero submission deficits.

Case A: all four native L<=1 and native mean upper95% bound<=1. H1 falsified
on this apparatus; no readiness change, investigate extension-owned failures.
Native floor repeated-high: all four native L>1 AND native mean lower95% bound>1.
Case B: repeated-high native, and a C/D paired penalty lower95% bound exceeds
either practical margin. Native sensitivity does not excuse added degradation.
Case C: repeated-high native, and B/C/D upper95% bounds meet both practical
limits, zero C/D submission deficits, no incompatible observer/safety evidence.
Only then may a reviewed prospective readiness ADR be proposed. Absolute safety
requirements still include >=58fps average and final120s for C/D, no errors,
unwanted owner/tier changes, clean foreground, exact parity and public scope.
Every one of the eight individual C/D sessions must pass both whole-window and
final120s submitted/presented>=58fps, zero application deficits and all safety
checks; arm averages cannot conceal a failure. Missing operands cannot pass.
Case D: any remaining mixed/uncertain pattern, including intervals crossing a
decision boundary. Remain PARTIAL. No best-run selection or post-hoc extension
of repetitions. A/B/C classification is apparatus-specific, not cross-vendor.
The0.5pp margin is an engineering degradation allowance, not preservation of the
old absolute1% ceiling or a perceptual guarantee. Case C only permits drafting
a new ADR, not READY. Any changed readiness rule needs independent approval
before fresh confirmatory evidence; these floor trials cannot be reclassified
retroactively as acceptance under an uncommitted rule.
