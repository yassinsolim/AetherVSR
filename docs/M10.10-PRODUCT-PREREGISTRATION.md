## Three Independent Questions

1. Acquisition: which browser-supported path supplies video and audio, with what
   origin privileges, pixel provenance, cadence and transport?
2. Presentation: a normal extension-origin tab or popup/window owns all layout,
   canvas and controls. Optional Document PiP cannot replace that primary surface.
3. Authority: exactly PAGE_AUTHORITY for mirrors or PLAYER_AUTHORITY for replay.
   Source selection, seeks and lifecycle have one canonical generation, not two
   competing players. Presentation cannot cure unusable acquisition or authority UX.

Mirror keeps page video/source selection, buffering, timeline, seeks and ABR
canonical. Media stream transport carries frames, not extension JSON. Bounded
typed control requests may address only the selected HTMLVideoElement. Replay owns
its own timeline; source pausing/muting needs a separate explicit reversible user
action. No automatic page pause/mute as a side effect of inspection.

instrumented audio or the owner cannot establish one audible path, retain UNRESOLVED.
No microphone/system-recording consent is assumed or automated.
