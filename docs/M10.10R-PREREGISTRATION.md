## 5. Digital Timing Contract

The primary quantity is **identified video frame-start media PTS relative to the
audio render-frontier media timeline**, at successful submission. It is not the
sample currently leaving a speaker, compositor presentation, or physical scanout.
Report absolute signed intervals and the no-neural control phase separately.
Neither `currentTime` precision nor `getOutputTimestamp` proves physical alignment.

Generate deterministic, non-looping1280x720 H.264/AAC progressive assets at30/60fps
with at least70s duration; generate a longer asset only if soak is eligible.
Video carries a monotonically increasing16-bit frame identity, its complement,
guards, and periodic timeline markers. Audio carries deterministic sample-addressed
impulses and an aperiodic low-amplitude identity signal. Recipes, seeds, codec/tool
versions and hashes are retained. No copyrighted source media is required.

Offline validation decodes ALL video identities and verifies frame number versus
integer container PTS. Audio uses decoded frame PTS plus sample offset, verifying
continuity, priming/skip/edit handling and full decoded length. Raw PCM index zero
is not assumed to mean media timestamp zero. Known positive/negative media-PTS
shifts test origin handling. Impulse identity is distinct from detected edge time.

The product-equivalent research audio graph is fixed before calibration:
MediaElementAudioSource -> gain/mute gate -> pass-through AudioWorklet -> output.
The worklet records actual `currentFrame`, block length, sample rate and finite
waveform diagnostics; message arrival is never an audio timestamp. The same graph,
sink and processing configuration is used for candidates. A diagnostic-only graph
cannot silently stand in for another product path. Muted/silent segments cannot
earn waveform timing coverage; state/epoch handling must report that explicitly.

Dense waveform correspondence against independently decoded PCM provides audio
media-time anchors. Each correspondence must be unique under fixed matching and
error criteria, with held-out samples and deliberate shifts. No interpolation
between sparse impulses may be called a hard bound. Between validated anchors use
an explicit monotonicity assumption and the enclosing timestamp interval; unknown
identity, discontinuity or unsupported rate invalidates the epoch. Report empirical
matching residuals separately from conservative enclosure width. Stop if the
enclosure required by the claim cannot be justified.

At each successful frame record element currentTime, full rVFC metadata available
in this browser (mediaTime, presentedFrames, presentationTime, expectedDisplayTime),
ordered main-clock/audio-currentTime brackets, submission sequence/generation and
actual source identity. Use a research-only Upscaler observer on the SAME imported
texture consumed by rendering, with preallocated GPU identity storage. No per-frame
CPU pixel readbacks, avoidable GPU allocation, or awaited import-to-submit gap.
Map identity records after pause; unsampled/ambiguous frames cannot inherit a
neighbor's identity. Existing production shaders, pipeline and controller stay
unchanged. Diagnostic shader/source is research-only and separately attributed.

For output readiness, successful queue-completion notification gives only an
upper endpoint; pre-submit time is the lower endpoint. It includes queued work
and callback delivery, not pure GPU duration. Keep these completion intervals
separate from timestamp-query GPU elapsed time and submission-phase A/V. Failed
submissions, old generations, lost devices or missing completion do not count as
ready output. Async notifications must never block the frame callback.

## 7. PLAYER_AUTHORITY and Audio State

States: SOURCE_ACTIVE -> PREPARING_REPLAY -> HANDOVER -> PLAYER_ACTIVE ->
RETURNING -> SOURCE_ACTIVE. Failures also use ABORTED or explicit
RECOVERY_REQUIRED. The logical timeline owner is never both page and player.
Preparation may decode/play only behind a closed audio gate; it is not authority.

Enter requires explicit invocation and an exclusive source-side lease bound to
the exact element, source identity/generation, source document, player document,
broker epoch and one-use transaction. A second same-source contender is rejected
without invalidating the first lease; independent sources may have independent
players. Unrelated videos are never paused or commanded. Select/reselect must not
mutate an active lease as a side effect.

Capture original page paused/muted/volume/rate at the source-side enter boundary.
Prepare and validate replay, seek near the page, then atomically revalidate and
pause ONLY the selected source. Read its final paused media time, reconcile the
player to that time, await readiness, start it behind the closed gate, then open
the player gate and commit PLAYER_ACTIVE. Acknowledgments carry transaction/phase;
lost messages are not permission to blindly repeat side effects.

Normal-operation audio gate overlap is zero: source gate is observably closed
before player gate opens. The prospective upper bound on the browser-observable
handover gap is250ms. Source audibility observations include paused/muted/volume/
currentTime; player observations include those fields plus actual graph gate and
nonzero audio-render samples. Distinguish a media-state gate endpoint from a
rendered-sample endpoint; do not label either speaker latency. If the chosen
instrument cannot bound the stated gap, the audio contract is UNRESOLVED, not PASS.
Initially paused/muted/zero-volume cases have explicit silent-state preservation,
not fabricated zero audio latency. Unexpected resumption of the exact page video
is an authority conflict: close player audio and surface recovery, never command
a replacement element. Independent page audio is outside this selected-video claim.

## 8. Return, Close and Rollback

Return is serialized and one-use. Close/pause player audio and snapshot its time
first; revalidate the exact original lease; seek that page video; restore ORIGINAL
page mute/volume/rate; resume iff it was playing at enter. If originally paused,
return paused. Player volume/mute changes are local and must not overwrite original
page choices. Restore only properties still owned by the transaction; conflicting
external page changes require explicit recovery rather than overwriting user state.
On success relinquish authority and release the player. No layout/hiding mutation.

Direct player Close leaves the page paused and does not attempt automatic Return.
This deliberate semantic is visible through the separate Return control. A stale
original source disables Return; never use a replacement video or a new document.

Before source pause, failure destroys preparation and leaves the source untouched.
After pause but before active commit, attempt bounded rollback to the exact source
and saved state. Return and rollback seek/start deadlines are2s per operation and
5s total. Lost/late acknowledgments are fenced by phase/generation and idempotent
transaction records. If rollback cannot be proved, preserve a usable recovery
surface with RECOVERY_REQUIRED. Do not leave both a dead player and silently
paused source. Failure injection covers fetch, load/decode, pre-handover seek,
immediately after source pause, player start, audio start and Return.

## 9. Capabilities, Invalidation and Permissions

The new research design separates complete media-byte ownership from remote
control/fetch authority. Source removal/replacement/src change/navigation/close or
broker-port loss permanently revokes the old Return and fetch capabilities. Fully
validated, committed owned bytes may keep playing under PLAYER_AUTHORITY. An
unfinished acquisition aborts. An old player never rebinds automatically to a new
worker/document. A source-side lease survives broker loss as a conflict fence;
it cannot confer new privilege or silently attach to another element.

Optional permission removal aborts uncommitted privileged fetch and forbids new
privileged refetch. Already committed bytes need no host privilege for playback.
Direct streaming without complete bytes does NOT earn that continuation guarantee.
Use the existing selected-URL/credential policy, one-use broker authorization,
pre/post identity and permission checks, byte/deadline limits and redirect:error.
Same-origin and outside-origin redirects are both rejected, with destination
requests observed. Credentials are omitted except the explicitly authenticated
fixture; response bytes/object URLs stay extension-owned, never sent to the page.

Native tests must exercise FINAL code: worker termination during PLAYER_ACTIVE,
authenticated same-document invalidation, and player A then B on separate selected
videos, close older A, verify B still protected by navigation/permission tracking.
Also reject a same-source contender without disturbing the active player. Tests
include stale/forged capabilities and delayed messages. Any P0/P1 invalidates the
affected native evidence; later repairs require fresh evidence with a new pin.
